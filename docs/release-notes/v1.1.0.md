### Upgrading from 1.0.0

Right-click the new `PocketPossum.scr` and choose **Install**, as before. If the tray app is running, exit it first
(right-click its tray icon, **Exit**) so the old file isn't locked. Your settings are kept.

### What's new

**Weather sync (optional)**
- Open **Settings > Weather**, tick *Sync the scene with the real weather*, search for your city and pick it.
- **Clouds** follow the real cloud cover (how many, how dark, how fast the wind moves them), day and night.
  The sun, moon and stars dim behind them.
- **Rain** falls slanted by the wind. **Snow** falls and settles on the trees and rail, in any season.
  **Fog** rolls in. **Thunderstorms** add lightning, and thunder if sounds are on. The possums get startled by the bolts.
- Fireflies and butterflies stay home when it is wet or foggy, and leaves and petals stay down in the rain.
- A small line under the clock shows the conditions and temperature, for example `6°C · Clear · Dublin`
  (Celsius, Fahrenheit or automatic by region).
- **Real sunrise and sunset** now drive day and night, and the **season and hemisphere** come from your chosen
  location. This also fixes the 1.0.0 limitation that day length did not change with the seasons.
- If the connection drops, the last reading is used for up to 3 hours, then the normal scene returns.

**Settings dialog**
- Reorganised into tabs: Scene, Time & season, Weather and Startup.

**Fixes**
- The live preview in the Windows Screen Saver dialog now uses your day/night and season settings instead of the defaults.

**For developers**
- An [Inno Setup](https://jrsoftware.org/isinfo.php) installer script is included (`installer/PocketPossum.iss`) and
  built by the GitHub Actions workflow. It is not part of this release yet because it has not been tested end to end.

### Privacy

Weather sync is **off by default**. When you turn it on, the screensaver sends the coordinates of the location you
chose, over HTTPS, to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) about every 15 minutes, and the city search sends the text
you type to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Nothing else is sent, and no account or API key is involved. Weather data by
[Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

### Known limitations

- The `.scr` is unsigned (see above).
- Bandit and the Maple family appear every night; there is no setting to turn them off yet.
- Weather sync needs an internet connection and uses an approximate current reading, not a forecast.
- Windows only (x64).
