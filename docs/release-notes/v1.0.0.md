## Possum Operational ScreenSaver (POSS) v1.0.0

The first release: a real Windows screensaver (`.scr`) starring Barnaby the possum, a moonlit porch, and a cast of
nocturnal troublemakers.

### Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (x64)
- [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0) (x64)
- WebView2 Runtime (preinstalled on Windows 11 and current Windows 10)

### Install

1. Download `PocketPossum.scr` (or unzip the zip).
2. Right-click it and choose **Install**. Windows opens Screen Saver Settings with it selected.
   Use **Settings...** to configure it. To install for all users, copy it to `C:\Windows\System32` first.
3. Optional: double-click `PocketPossum.scr` with no arguments to run it in the system tray, where it starts the
   screensaver after your chosen idle time.

### What's in 1.0.0

**A real screensaver**
- Runs on every monitor, exits on any mouse or keyboard input, and shows a live preview in the Windows dialog.
- Works offline: the scene, CSS and fonts are embedded in the single ~1.5 MB file.
- Settings dialog for scene, clock, sounds and more, saved per user.

**Time and weather**
- **Day and night** follow your system clock with a smooth sunrise/sunset blend. You choose when day and night start.
- **Four seasons** follow today's date in your time zone (hemisphere inferred from the zone, or set it yourself):
  spring petals, summer butterflies and bees, autumn leaves, winter snow.
- Scenes: Porch, OLED Void (pure black, always night) and Synthwave.

**Idle timer**
- A tray app starts the screensaver after N minutes with no keyboard or mouse activity (default 10).
- Optional "run in the tray when Windows starts" (per-user, no admin needed).

**The possums**
- **Barnaby** patrols the porch rail and wire, sniffs, grooms, naps, hangs by his tail, plays dead and raids the trash can.
- After dark, **Bandit** wanders in and causes chaos, followed shortly by **Maple** and her three joeys,
  **Pip**, **Nibs** and **Dot**.
- They chase, hiss and faint, toss and steal snacks, wreck the trash can, swipe the baby joey, hitch rides on Maple's
  back and nap in a pile. It is all random, so no two nights are the same. They head home at dawn.

**Burn-in protection**
- The clock wanders the screen, the moon travels across the sky and the whole scene drifts slowly.

### Known limitations

- The `.scr` is unsigned (see above).
- The extra possums (Bandit and the family) appear every night; there is no setting to turn them off yet.
- Day and night switch at fixed hours you choose; day length does not change with the seasons.
- Windows only (x64).

### Troubleshooting

Diagnostics, including page script errors and what the possums are up to, are written to
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\PocketPossum\log.txt`. Settings live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PocketPossum\settings.json`.
