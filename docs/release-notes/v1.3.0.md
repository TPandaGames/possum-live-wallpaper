### Upgrading

Exit the tray app first (right-click its tray icon, **Exit**) so the old file isn't locked, then install the new
`PocketPossum.scr` as before (right-click, **Install**). Your settings are kept, and the possum stays the default.

### What's new

**Animal choice**
- Pick **Possum**, **Cat**, **Raccoon**, **Dog** or **Lizard** in **Settings > Scene > Animal**.
- Each has its own look: a tabby cat with pointed ears, a masked raccoon with a ringed tail, a floppy-eared dog that
  pants and wags, and a long-tailed lizard with a flicking tongue.
- The whole cast changes with it: the lead animal, the troublemaker, the mother and her three babies (kittens, kits,
  puppies or hatchlings), each with their own names, for example Buddy, Scamp and Daisy for dogs.
- Behaviour is shared, so every species patrols, naps, chases, holds hissing standoffs, steals snacks, rides on the
  mother's back and gets startled by thunder. On-screen messages use the right names and words.
- Works in the screensaver, the live wallpaper and the preview.

### Known limitations

- The other animals keep the possum's schedule: they arrive after dark and doze more by day. A cat, dog or lizard
  "hangs" from the wire stretched out by its legs, the way the possum hangs by its tail.
- The second animal and the family appear every night; there is no setting to turn them off yet.
- Multiple monitors are untested, and the live wallpaper is only tested with a possum. The `.scr` is unsigned.
- Windows only (x64).

Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/TPandaGames/possum-live-wallpaper#readme).
